Global travel marketplace · Lead scoring

Problem
Marketing spent evenly across leads, though most were unlikely to convert.
Approach
Built a propensity model (logistic regression through random forest to XGBoost), handled class imbalance, and tuned the score threshold against cost and revenue rather than accuracy alone.
Outcome
Spend on low-propensity leads fell, cutting marketing cost by 30% while lead quality rose 20%. The team also gained cohort LTV forecasts and weekly executive dashboards for budget decisions.
Millionsof users scored for churn and targeted across email, push and in-app

Global ed-tech platform · Lifecycle & CRM

Problem
Retention campaigns reached everyone alike, and campaign impact was hard to separate from natural behaviour.
Approach
Churn propensity models (XGBoost, LightGBM) on Databricks, K-Means and RFM segments, automated feature pipelines for audience generation, plus holdout and lift experiments read with Bayesian and frequentist methods.
Outcome
Leadership could target retention spend by risk and expected uplift, make go/no-go calls on experiments, and build new audiences far faster. An LLM agent over governed data let stakeholders run their own root-cause analysis.
15%revenue improvement
30% faster reporting

Property marketplace · Growth analytics

Problem
Spend wasn’t tied to which channels and cohorts actually paid back, and the funnel had unexplained drop-offs.
Approach
Channel-level ROAS, cohort LTV and payback analysis, plus funnel segmentation that located the worst drop-off at the “contact agent” step.
Outcome
Spend shifted to top-performing channels and the drop-off led to a UX redesign and an agent response-time SLA. Automated Python reporting cut turnaround by 30%.
